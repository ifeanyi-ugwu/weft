## Name

Weft is always written lowercase in the wordmark (`weft`) and capitalised in running text ("Weft"). In a weave, the **warp** threads are fixed on the loom and the **weft** passes through them. Here the network is the warp; your identity and transactions are the weft.

When referring to Fabric, use the tagline: **weft — an identity wallet for Hyperledger Fabric**. Keep "Hyperledger" and "Fabric" out of the name and logo; they're Linux Foundation trademarks.

## Mark

Three weft threads pass over and under two warp threads. At 24px and below, the weave drops away and the threads become three bars.

Amber and Signal carry meaning. Don't use them for decoration, or people stop noticing them.

## Type

- **IBM Plex Sans**: interface text and explanations.
- **IBM Plex Mono**: anything a user might copy or compare (MSP IDs, transaction IDs, fingerprints, function names, arguments) and the wordmark.

Tokens for both, plus spacing and radii, are in `packages/brand/tokens.css` and `tokens.json`.
